This site is a personal troubleshooting knowledge base. It is intended for capturing:

- recurring errors that are easy to forget
- environment setup steps that take too long to rediscover
- root-cause and verification notes for bugs

When a note stops being useful, archive or rewrite it. The goal is fast lookup, not perfect completeness.

## Structure

- Topic pages such as [Android](/android/), [Linux](/linux/), and [Network](/network/) hold evergreen reference notes.
- Posts under `_posts/` are for dated troubleshooting sessions and one-off debugging histories.
- The left navigation keeps the topic index and recent logs visible from every page.
